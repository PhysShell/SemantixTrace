# 06 — E4: Native semantic events

Status: **not executed — gate not passed.**

The research contract gates E4 on E2/E3 justifying continuation. They do
not: H1 (representation) and H2 (graph/oracle) were falsified on both
development datasets with effect sizes of tens of percentage points in
the negative direction, and the frozen kill criteria were met
(`04-e2-representation.md`, `05-e3-algorithm.md`).

Running an instrumentation study (O vs O+S) on top of a pipeline whose
representation demonstrably destroys the baseline's signal would measure
noise, not the value of native semantics. The E4 question — whether
explicit domain events add diagnostic signal beyond ordinary
observability — remains **open and untested**; it becomes worth funding
only after (and if) a causally-grouped canonical representation exists
and passes an E2-style representation check.
